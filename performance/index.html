Leads are HubSpot contacts created on or after June 1 with LinkedIn paid social as the original source and a LinkedIn lead gen form as a conversion. Internal Visme test submissions are left out. For CPL, use Campaign Manager lead counts rather than these.

Dark funnel accounts match a demo requester's email domain to the website on the LinkedIn company page Fibbler saw engaging. Domains shared by several LinkedIn pages (league sites like nhl.com, careers sites) are skipped so a match is never ambiguous. Demo requests are the Main Sales Form, Support to Sales form and ABM landing page forms, dated from HubSpot's first or most recent conversion on the contact, so a demo request sandwiched between other form fills is not picked up. Any contact at the account whose first or most recent conversion was a LinkedIn lead gen form before the request removes the account. Engagement is company level, so it shows the account was paying attention, not that the requester personally saw an ad.

Spend comes from Fibbler's sync of LinkedIn billing. Budget caps and pacing live in Campaign Manager.

Pipeline and closed won use Fibbler's company-level influence model: a deal counts if anyone at the buying company saw a LinkedIn ad in the 12 months before it was created (pipeline) or closed (won). Fibbler's pipeline figure includes open and closed lost deals. These deals are not limited to the leads above, and expansion deals are included.
